SKY RUNNER

Fly the canyon. Thread every ring. Don't kiss the mountain.

You are the test pilot of a little stunt plane over a wild island: snowy peaks to the north, open sea to the south, and a sleepy harbour town in between. Twelve glowing rings hang in the sky along the course. Fly through each one to score — the faster and cleaner the run, the bigger the bonus. Touch rock, water or a rooftop and the run is over. Beat the clock, beat your own best score.
